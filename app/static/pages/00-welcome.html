Welcome

This course trains clinicians who are not radiologists to perform screening liver ultrasound for liver lesions suspicious for possible carcinoma. The goal is not to train or expect you to diagnose on ultrasound, but rather to train you to provide quick point-of-care ultrasound in patients who have a higher pretest probability of having a suspicious lesion.

01Reading and things to complete before Session 1

  • AASLD 2023 Practice Guidance on HCC, the surveillance section

The Pretest. Forty questions. You are not expected to know this material yet and a low score costs you nothing, it is a baseline, taken again at the end so the program can measure what the course actually taught. You will not see the answers until it closes. The posttest would otherwise measure how well you remembered a feedback screen, not what you actually learned.

The Confidence Survey. Seven questions about how confident you feel, not what you know. Answer honestly in both the pre- and post-course survey, since your answers will be important for revising this course.

03What to expect from the didactic weeks

The session pages are long. Each ends with a section quiz you can retake as often as you like, which is for your own knowledge and not for grading, and the rationale for every answer appears once you submit.

One request When something in these pages does not match what you see on a real patient, or what local practice does, say so. This curriculum is new, it was written largely from guidelines produced elsewhere, and the people best placed to catch what does not transfer are the people using it here. Please contact us with anything you find incorrect or confusing.
